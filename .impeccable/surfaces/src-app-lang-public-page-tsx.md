# Landing page (/) — surface brief

## Scope and mode
Public landing page plus the shared Log in / Sign up dialog (bottom sheet on phones). Mode: Persuade. A responsive web page, not an app: real web layout on desktop (top nav with section links, two-column hero, wide grids), Flo-style softness at phone widths. Never implies an app download.

## Audience, job, action
- Audience: pregnant women in Barangay Taysan on modern phones. Staff only use "Log in".
- Must understand within seconds: never miss a checkup (SMS reminders); easy to join (phone browser, no email needed); her pregnancy and records on her phone; run by her own health center.
- Primary action: "Sign up" (opens the sign-up dialog). Secondary: "Log in" (header, closing, footer).
- Content: weeks slider estimate, 5 activity types, how it works with a sample SMS thread, sample "My visits", health center (address Brgy. 56 Taysan, Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM, contact to be announced), healthy habits + danger signs (not medical advice), closing call to action, footer with seal, links, privacy and credits.
- Proof: no testimonials, numbers or invented claims. Sample data is labelled as sample.
- User feedback that shaped it: wanted Flo's soft, cute icon feel; rejected the phone mock-up hero and realistic 3D renders as harsh; found the date box a hassle; asked for bolder titles and strict alignment between paired cards.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A soft, Flo-like welcome that centres the mother: a gentle illustrated mother in a rose arch with a glowing baby glyph, and the system's real job (weeks, SMS reminder, next visit) floating around her. It refuses the generic SaaS hero (headline + device screenshot + feature grid) and refuses clinical realism.
OWN-WORLD: Client palette (leaf/fern greens, beige, sage, cream, milk ground) plus one dusty-rose accent for hearts, the baby glyph, danger signs and soft tints. Inter only, extra-bold titles, Flo's type scale. Rounded white cards (20–36px), pill buttons, Phosphor icons inside soft colour circles, Health Icons fetus glyph, curved rose hero, soft low shadows, one gentle float motion on the hero cards and SMS badge.
STORY: She reads "Never miss a checkup again", drags "How far along are you?", sees her trimester and due month and the weeks card on the illustration update, learns how reminders work from a real-looking SMS thread, checks the services and her health center, and taps Sign up in the same dialog style she will log in with.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: header (seal + name, section links, EN/FIL, Log in, Sign up); left: extra-bold headline (up to 56px), lead, white slider card (weeks value, −/+ and track with trimester labels, trimester + due month tiles, estimate note), Sign up pill, "works in your phone's browser" line; right: mother illustration in a rose arch with three floating cards (weeks + baby glyph top right, SMS left, next visit bottom right). Phones/tablets: headline, lead, illustration with only the weeks card, slider card, full-width Sign up.
FORM: Pinned by the user (Flo-inspired; approved style preview 2026-10-27); iterated live with the user; no concept-seed roll. Signature interaction: the weeks slider driving the floating weeks card and baby glyph.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented until the finish reviewer returns ship and the documenter rewrites DESIGN.md.
